Case Study: PesaSmart App

Project Information

  • Category: Mobile App & Fintech
  • Client: PesaSmart Kenya Ltd
  • Project Date: August 2026
  • Tech Stack: Django REST, Flutter, PostgreSQL

Empowering Financial Literacy in Nairobi

PesaSmart approached Fahari Digital to develop an automated expense tracker designed specifically for Kenyans. By automatically categorizing M-Pesa SMS alerts and providing real-time monthly budget charts, the app achieved over 45,000 active monthly users within 90 days of launch.

Engineered with end-to-end encryption and biometric authentication, PesaSmart represents the highest tier of Kenyan financial software engineering.
