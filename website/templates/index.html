Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about working with Fahari Digital? Here are the answers to what our clients ask most.

Are you based in Nairobi?

Yes! Our main office is in Nairobi, Kenya. We work with clients locally across Kenya and remotely across East Africa and beyond. We're fluent in both Swahili and English and always available on WhatsApp, email, and Zoom.

Do you integrate M-Pesa and other local payment methods?

Absolutely. M-Pesa (Daraja API), Airtel Money, and card payments are part of our standard toolkit. We have built and deployed payment integrations for dozens of Kenyan businesses and understand the nuances of Safaricom's APIs inside and out.

How much does a website or app cost in Kenya?

Our Starter packages begin at KSh 25,000/month, covering a professional 5-page responsive website. Full web applications and mobile apps are quoted based on scope. We always provide a detailed, transparent quote before any work begins — no surprises.

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website takes 2–4 weeks. A full custom web application takes 4–12 weeks depending on complexity. We provide weekly progress updates and use collaborative tools so you're always in the loop throughout the process.

Do you work with NGOs and government organisations?

Yes! We have experience working with NGOs, county government digital initiatives, and social enterprises across Kenya. We understand procurement processes and can provide all necessary documentation including KRA PIN certificates, CR12, and compliance letters.
