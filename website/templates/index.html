About Us

Fahari Digital is an independent software development agency based in Westlands, Nairobi. We help local enterprises, growing companies, and non-profit organisations build custom digital tools.

  • Local payment integration with Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja and bank portals.
  • Custom web applications developed with Python, Django, and modern JavaScript.
  • Direct access to the engineers who write and maintain your code.

We build software designed for everyday Kenyan conditions. That means pages that load quickly on mobile networks, layouts that work on budget smartphones, and checkout flows that integrate smoothly with local mobile money. You work directly with our core engineering team from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from clients who work with us.

Where are you located?

Our office is located along Waiyaki Way in Westlands, Nairobi. We hold in-person meetings at our office or coffee spots around Nairobi, and video meetings over Google Meet or Zoom.

Can you connect M-Pesa to an existing website?

Yes. We configure Safaricom Daraja STK Push and Paybill notifications on custom Python Django backends, PHP sites, and common content platforms.

How do project payments work?

We split project costs into three stages: a 40% initial deposit to begin work, 40% once the demo site is approved on our staging server, and the remaining 20% on final launch.

Do we own the website code once complete?

Yes. You own 100% of your source code, domain name, and server credentials once the final payment is cleared. We hand over the complete GitHub repository to your account.

Can you help us with domain registration and hosting?

Yes. We can purchase and configure .co.ke or .com domains through licensed Kenyan registrars and set up hosting on fast cloud servers.
